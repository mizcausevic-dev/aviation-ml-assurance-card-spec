# Public sources and status

Checked 2026-10-12. These are sources for terminology and policy context, not endorsements of this specification.

| Source | Status and use |
|---|---|
| [EASA NPA 2025-07](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/notices-of-proposed-amendment/npa-2025-07) | **Notice of Proposed Amendment**, not final certification rules. Context for AI trustworthiness, Level 1/2 assistance and teaming, learning assurance, data management, and human factors. |
| [EASA Concept Paper, Proposed Issue 3](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/general-publications/easa-artificial-intelligence-concept-paper-proposed-issue-3) | **Proposed concept paper**, exploring Level 3 advanced automation. It does not establish certification of any system described in a card. |
| [FAA Roadmap for AI Safety Assurance](https://www.faa.gov/aircraft/air_cert/step/roadmap_for_AI_safety_assurance) | FAA roadmap and guiding principles. The roadmap says its actions do not dictate certification specifics. |

`SAE ARP6983`, `EUROCAE ED-324`, `SAE ARP4754B`, and `RTCA DO-178C` are cited by identifier only where relevant. Their licensed text and any unverified objective mapping are excluded from this repository.
